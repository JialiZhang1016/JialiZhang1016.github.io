AI News Agent Workflow

iFlytek Strategy Department | Summers 2024–2026

Automated a daily brief that previously took about one hour of manual work; reached 200+ business readers.

What I built

  • Built and deployed a scheduled Python pipeline for iFlytek's Strategy Department. It collects AI news from RSS and web sources, then removes duplicate URLs, similar titles, and items already in local or Feishu history.
  • Used Claude Code, with Codex as a fallback, for source review, relevance scoring, title translation, evidence-based summaries, and classification. Python handles scheduling, deduplication, structured-output parsing, score filtering, and delivery.
  • Automated a daily brief that had taken about one hour of manual work each day; shortened weekly reporting from two hours to 30 minutes. The briefs reached more than 200 readers across the business.

How I would adapt it for Skyworks

Starting from an engineering question defined by the team, an agent could search accessible RF and acoustic-device papers, organize candidates, and extract methods, supporting evidence, and limitations with citations. Device engineers would review the shortlist and decide which ideas merit a validation test. This lower workflow is a proposal, not a system I claim to have deployed at Skyworks.
