AI News Agent Workflow

iFlytek Strategy Department | Summers 2024–2026

Automated a daily brief that previously took about one hour of manual work; reached 200+ business readers.

What I built

  • Built and deployed a scheduled Python pipeline for iFlytek's Strategy Department. It collects AI news from RSS and web sources, then removes duplicate URLs, similar titles, and items already in local or Feishu history.
  • Used Claude Code, with Codex as a fallback, for source review, relevance scoring, title translation, evidence-based summaries, and classification. Python handles scheduling, deduplication, structured-output parsing, score filtering, and delivery.
  • Automated a daily brief that had taken about one hour of manual work each day; shortened weekly reporting from two hours to 30 minutes. The briefs reached more than 200 readers across the business. For the weekly leadership brief, I checked the AI draft's sources, translations, and priorities before sharing it.

How I would adapt it for Skyworks

I would build a recurring literature scan around topics the Acoustic Device Group cares about, such as SAW and BAW resonators, RF filter performance, ANN surrogate models, and electromagnetic or multiphysics modeling. The agent could search IEEE Xplore and arXiv, use Crossref DOI metadata to organize and deduplicate papers, then screen abstracts through approved APIs. For promising papers, it would assess methods, data, validation evidence, and limitations from open or properly licensed full text where available. Engineers would check the citations and select items for a concise, cited weekly brief with follow-up questions. This is a proposed workflow, not a system I claim to have deployed at Skyworks.
