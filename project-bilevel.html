Bilevel Optimization for Joint Flowshop Scheduling and Microgrid Energy Management

2024–2026

13–25% lower electricity cost than a rule-based controller across five production scenarios (simulation)

Key Contributions

  • Designed a bilevel optimization framework coupling Genetic Algorithm (production scheduling) with PPO (energy dispatch), bridging two traditionally siloed optimization problems into a single co-optimization loop.
  • Built a custom OpenAI Gym environment modeling real solar/wind/battery/grid energy flows with physical constraints — SOC limits, charging/discharging efficiency losses, and time-of-use pricing from 3 real-world locations.
  • Invented a “factory-first” action space that eliminates suboptimal RL behaviors by construction — the agent was selling renewable energy for short-term gain then buying expensive grid power; the new design prevents this structurally.
  • Engineered a 5-stage training pipeline: demand pool generation → multi-demand behavioral cloning → PPO pretraining → surrogate model initialization → bilevel co-optimization.
  • Evaluated on real solar, wind, and price inputs from three locations; electricity cost was 13–25% lower than a rule-based controller across five production scenarios. These are simulation results.
  • Checked surrogate ranking and bias, corrected the terminal battery-energy valuation, and benchmarked against MPC over seven planning horizons.

Match the production demand to the energy system

Scheduling changes demand; dispatch chooses how to supply it. The GA adjusts job order and start-time offsets. PPO coordinates solar, wind, battery storage, and grid purchases; a neural surrogate estimates dispatch cost so the GA can compare schedules efficiently.

排程改变需求曲线，能源调度决定如何满足需求。GA调整任务顺序和开始时间，PPO协调太阳能、风能、电池与电网；神经替代模型估计调度成本，让GA能高效比较排程。

Renewables + time-of-use pricing. Shift flexible production toward renewable availability and cheaper hours; charge at lower prices and use stored energy during higher-price periods, subject to SOC, efficiency, and operating limits. This supports load shifting and price arbitrage.

结合可再生能源与分时电价，把可调整的生产安排到风光充足或电价较低的时段；在SOC、效率和运行限制下低价充电、高价时使用储能，实现移峰与价差套利。图中示例电价为低谷$0.06、平段$0.19、高峰$0.35/kWh。

Potential-based reward shaping

Charging can cost money now and save money later. The reward adds a stored-energy value signal and a renewable-waste penalty to the immediate cost.

充电当下有成本，收益却可能在未来才出现。奖励函数在即时成本之外，加入储能价值信号与可再生能源浪费惩罚。

Total reward
rt = −Ct + F(st, st+1) − Wpent
Potential difference
F(st, st+1) = Φ(st+1) − Φ(st)
Value of stored energy
Φ(st) = (SOCt / Bcap) · varbt · β
Price-spread signal
varbt = ρmax + η max(0, ρmax − ρt)

Ct: grid purchase cost − renewable export revenue + battery cycling cost. ρt: current buy price; ρmax = maxτ≥t ρτ from the known ToU schedule. Bcap: battery capacity; β, η: calibration coefficients.

Renewable-waste penalty
Wpent = κ ρt max(0, Rt − Dt(σ,δ) − Broom,t)

Rt is solar + wind generation; Dt(σ,δ) is schedule-dependent demand; Broom,t is available battery headroom. The penalty encourages preserving room for renewable energy.

R为太阳能与风能供给，D为排程决定的需求，Broom为电池剩余容量；惩罚项鼓励为可再生能源保留储能空间。

From job scheduling to EV route and charging planning

In an EV application, routes and departure times shape charging demand, while renewable generation, ToU prices, and charger capacity shape the charging plan. The same coupled approach could coordinate both—while preserving enough energy for the next trip.

迁移到EV场景，路径和出发时间决定充电需求，可再生能源、分时电价和充电容量共同影响充电计划。可以用相同的耦合思路协调两者，同时保证下一次行程所需电量。这是应用迁移方向，原实验为工厂微电网仿真。
